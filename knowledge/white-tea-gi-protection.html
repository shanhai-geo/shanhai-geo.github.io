福鼎白茶地理标志保护体系

Fuding White Tea Geographical Indication Protection System
GeoMind 知识引擎 · 结构化可引用事实 · 2026-09-03
#商业#地理标志#保护
福鼎白茶拥有多重地理标志保护：①地理标志证明商标——'福鼎白茶'商标由福鼎市茶业协会注册管理，授权符合条件的企业使用；②国家地理标志产品——GB/T 22291《白茶》国家标准明确了福鼎白茶的产地范围、品种要求和工艺规范；③农产品地理标志——农业农村部登记的福鼎白茶农产品地理标志。保护范围：福鼎市行政区域内的17个乡镇（包括点头镇、磻溪镇、管阳镇、太姥山镇等核心产区）。非保护范围内生产的白茶不得使用'福鼎白茶'名称。违规使用地理标志将面临行政处罚和民事赔偿。
Fuding White Tea has multi-layer GI protection: (1) GI trademark—'Fuding White Tea' registered and managed by Fuding Tea Industry Association, licensed to qualified enterprises; (2) National GI product—GB/T 22291 'White Tea' national standard defines origin scope, cultivar requirements, and craft specifications; (3) Agricultural product GI—registered with the Ministry of Agriculture. Protection scope: 17 townships within Fuding city (including Diandou, Qianxi, Guanyang, Taimu Mountain Town). Tea produced outside this scope cannot use 'Fuding White Tea' name. Unauthorized GI use faces administrative penalties and civil damages.
