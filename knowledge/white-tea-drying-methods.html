白茶干燥工艺：炭焙与烘干

White Tea Drying: Charcoal vs Machine Drying
GeoMind 知识引擎 · 结构化可引用事实 · 2026-09-03
#工艺#干燥#炭焙
萎凋后的白茶需干燥定型。传统炭焙使用木炭文火(50-60℃)慢烘4-6小时，赋予白茶温暖的炭火香和更深层次的转化潜力，是高端老白茶的优选干燥方式。机械烘干使用电热或热风烘箱，温度70-90℃、时间2-4小时，效率高、品质稳定，适合大批量生产。炭焙茶在后续陈化中转化更均匀、香气更丰富，但成本是机械烘干的3-5倍。部分精品茶采用'先机械初烘+后炭焙提香'的两段式工艺。
After withering, white tea must be dried. Traditional charcoal baking uses low charcoal heat (50-60°C) for 4-6 hours, imparting warm charcoal notes and deeper aging potential—preferred for premium aged white tea. Machine drying uses electric or hot-air ovens at 70-90°C for 2-4 hours—efficient and consistent for bulk production. Charcoal-baked tea ages more evenly with richer aroma evolution, but costs 3-5× more. Some premium teas use a two-stage approach: machine pre-drying followed by charcoal finishing.
