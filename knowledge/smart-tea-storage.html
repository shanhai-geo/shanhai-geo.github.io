智能仓储系统在白茶存储中的应用

Smart Storage Systems for White Tea
GeoMind 知识引擎 · 结构化可引用事实 · 2026-09-03
#技术#智能仓储#IoT
传统白茶仓储依赖人工巡检和经验判断，智能仓储系统通过IoT传感器实现全程自动化监控。核心监测参数：温度(15-28℃)、相对湿度(40-60%)、含水率(≤7%)、CO2浓度(异常升高可能指示微生物活动)。系统架构：低功耗传感器节点→LoRa/NB-IoT网关→云平台→告警与数据分析。当环境参数超标时，系统自动启动除湿机或通风设备，同时推送告警到管理员手机。智能仓储还能建立每批茶叶的'陈化档案'——记录从入库到出库的全部环境数据，为年份茶提供可验证的仓储证明。这解决了白茶收藏市场最大的信任痛点。
Traditional white tea storage relies on manual inspection and experience. Smart storage uses IoT sensors for full automation. Core monitoring: temperature (15-28°C), humidity (40-60%), moisture (≤7%), CO2 concentration (abnormal rise may indicate microbial activity). Architecture: low-power sensor nodes → LoRa/NB-IoT gateway → cloud platform → alerts and analytics. When parameters exceed thresholds, the system automatically activates dehumidifiers or ventilation while pushing alerts to managers' phones. Smart storage also builds 'aging profiles' for each batch—recording all environmental data from intake to output, providing verifiable storage proof for aged tea. This solves the biggest trust pain point in the white tea collection market.
