室内萎凋与加温萎凋工艺

Indoor and Heated Withering Methods
GeoMind 知识引擎 · 结构化可引用事实 · 2026-09-03
#工艺#室内萎凋#加温萎凋
室内萎凋在通风良好的萎凋房中进行，鲜叶摊放于多层架上，依靠自然通风萎凋48-72小时，品质接近日晒但产量更稳定。加温萎凋（也称人工萎凋）在控温控湿的萎凋槽中进行，温度设定30-38℃，时间缩短至24-36小时，适合大规模生产。加温萎凋效率高但香气层次不如日晒和室内自然萎凋丰富。多数规模化工厂采用'复式萎凋'——先日晒2-3小时再转入室内完成剩余走水，兼顾效率与品质。
Indoor withering takes place in well-ventilated rooms with leaves on multi-tier racks, relying on natural airflow for 48-72 hours—quality approaches sun-withered but with more stable output. Heated withering uses temperature/humidity-controlled troughs at 30-38°C for 24-36 hours, ideal for large-scale production. It is efficient but lacks the aroma complexity of natural methods. Most factories use 'combined withering'—2-3 hours of sun followed by indoor completion—balancing efficiency and quality.
