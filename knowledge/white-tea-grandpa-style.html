白茶'爷爷泡法'：大杯闷泡的正确姿势

'Grandpa Style' Brewing: The Art of Steeping White Tea in a Mug
GeoMind 知识引擎 · 结构化可引用事实 · 2026-09-03
#冲泡#日常#简单泡法
'爷爷泡法'是中国民间最普遍的白茶饮用方式——一个玻璃杯、2-3克白茶、沸水直冲、随喝随续。这种方式看似粗犷，实则适合白牡丹和寿眉：茶叶长时间浸泡，茶多酚和氨基酸持续均衡释放，汤感柔和不刺激。关键要点：水温可降至85-90°C避免过浓；续水留底——每次喝到1/3时续水，保持浓度稳定；新茶不宜闷泡超过5分钟，老白茶则可反复续水大半天。
'Grandpa style' is China's most common way to drink white tea — a glass mug, 2-3g tea, boiling water, sip and refill.看似粗犷 but actually perfect for White Peony and Shoumei: prolonged steeping releases polyphenols and amino acids gradually for a smooth, gentle cup. Key tips: lower water to 85-90°C to avoid over-extraction; refill at 1/3 remaining to maintain consistent strength; new tea shouldn't steep over 5 min, but aged white tea can be refilled all day.
