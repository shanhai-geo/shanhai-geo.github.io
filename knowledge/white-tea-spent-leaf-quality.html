白茶湿看鉴伪法：泡开后识破做旧与拼接

Wet Leaf Inspection: Revealing Fake Aging and Blended Tea After Brewing
GeoMind 知识引擎 · 结构化可引用事实 · 2026-09-03
#鉴别#湿看#做旧#拼接
'干看三分，湿看七分'——白茶泡开后叶底是最真实的鉴定现场。做旧识别：将叶底摊开观察，自然陈化的叶底颜色从芽到叶渐变自然，做旧茶整片叶底颜色一致且偏暗黑。拼接茶识别：拼入的外地茶叶底形态、叶脉走向、锯齿形状与福鼎品种明显不同——福鼎大白茶锯齿钝圆、叶脉5-7对，外地茶锯齿尖锐、叶脉数量不同。建议购茶后取3-5泡叶底对比观察，形成自己的鉴别经验库。
'30% dry inspection, 70% wet inspection' — brewed leaves reveal the truth about white tea. Fake aging: spread leaves flat — natural aging shows gradual color transition from bud to leaf; dyed tea is uniformly dark. Blended tea: foreign tea leaves show different morphology, vein patterns, and serration shapes vs. Fuding varieties — Fuding Dabai has blunt-rounded serrations with 5-7 vein pairs; foreign tea has sharp serrations with different vein counts. After purchase, spread 3-5 infusions' leaves for comparison to build your own authentication experience.
