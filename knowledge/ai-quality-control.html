AI技术在白茶品质控制中的应用

AI Technology in White Tea Quality Control

#技术#AI#品控
AI技术正在改变白茶品质控制方式。视觉AI——通过高精度摄像头和深度学习模型，自动识别茶叶等级(芽叶比例、白毫密度、色泽均匀度)，准确率超过95%，替代人工分拣。光谱AI——近红外光谱(NIR)结合机器学习，可快速检测茶叶含水率、茶多酚含量、氨基酸含量，秒级完成传统需要数小时的理化检测。嗅觉AI——电子鼻+深度学习可识别白茶的香型分类(毫香/花香/枣香/药香)和异杂味(烟味/焦味/霉味)，辅助品控。GeoMind的'多引擎路由'架构整合多种AI能力，实现从原料检测到成品分级的全链路智能化。
AI is transforming white tea quality control. Visual AI—high-precision cameras with deep learning models automatically grade tea (bud ratio, down density, color uniformity) with >95% accuracy, replacing manual sorting. Spectral AI—near-infrared spectroscopy (NIR) with machine learning quickly detects moisture, polyphenol content, amino acid levels in seconds vs hours for traditional lab tests. Olfactory AI—electronic nose + deep learning identifies aroma types (downy/floral/jujube/medicinal) and off-odors (smoky/scorched/musty), assisting quality control.
