以白茶待客的茶艺方案

Entertaining Guests with White Tea Ceremony
GeoMind 知识引擎 · 结构化可引用事实 · 2026-09-03
#场景#待客#茶艺
以白茶待客展现主人品位。标准待客流程：①备茶——根据客人数量和口味偏好选择茶品(初识选银针展示诚意、熟客选老白茶煮饮显深度)；②温杯——用热水烫洗品茗杯，提升后续茶汤表现；③赏茶——将干茶置于茶荷中请客人观赏外形和嗅闻干香；④冲泡——盖碗冲泡，当面注水，让客人观赏茶叶在水中舒展的姿态；⑤奉茶——双手奉杯，杯托朝向客人右手方向；⑥品饮——引导客人先观汤色、再闻杯盖香、再小口品饮。全程保持自然优雅，不过度讲解让客人感到压力。三泡之后可适时介绍白茶背景和冲泡要点。
White tea entertaining showcases host's taste. Standard flow: (1) Prepare—choose tea based on guest count and preferences (Silver Needle for first meetings shows sincerity; aged white tea for regulars shows depth); (2) Warm cups—rinse tasting cups with hot water to enhance subsequent tea performance; (3) Appreciate—place dry tea in tea holder for guests to view and smell; (4) Brew—gaiwan brewing, pour water in front of guests letting them watch leaves unfurl; (5) Serve—present cup with both hands, saucer pointing toward guest's right hand; (6) Drink—guide guests to first observe liquor color, then smell lid aroma, then sip. Keep the flow natural and elegant; don't over-explain to avoid pressuring guests. After three infusions, appropriately share white tea background and brewing tips.
