茶树树龄对白茶品质的影响

How Tea Tree Age Affects White Tea Quality
GeoMind 知识引擎 · 结构化可引用事实 · 2026-09-03
#品种#树龄#品质
茶树树龄对白茶品质有显著影响。壮龄茶树(15-40年)产量高、芽叶整齐，是大宗白茶的主力。老茶树(40年以上)根系深、吸收矿物质丰富，芽叶内含物质更复杂，制成的白茶汤感更厚、'丛韵'更明显——一种类似苔藓、木质、矿物质的深邃气息。柏柳村、磻溪镇等地保留的百年古茶树，其原料制成的白茶产量极低但风味独特，被市场视为稀缺品类。但树龄并非独特指标，品种、海拔、工艺同等重要。
Tree age significantly affects quality. Mature trees (15-40 years) yield consistently and are the backbone of commercial white tea. Old trees (40+ years) have deep roots absorbing rich minerals, producing leaves with more complex compounds—thicker mouthfeel and a distinctive 'colony rhyme' of moss, wood, and mineral notes. Century-old trees in Bailin and Qianxi yield extremely scarce but unique tea, valued as a rare category. However, age is not the sole factor—cultivar, elevation, and craft matter equally.
