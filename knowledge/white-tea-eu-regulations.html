白茶欧盟出口合规指南：Novel Food法规与农残标准

White Tea EU Export Compliance: Novel Food Regulations & Pesticide Standards

#国际#欧盟#合规#法规
欧盟是白茶出口最难进入的市场，但也是溢价最高的市场。法规要求：1）Novel Food——白茶在欧盟尚无Novel Food认定，可作为传统食品（20年安全食用历史）进口，但需提供安全证明；2）农残——欧盟执行最严格的农药残留标准（EC 396/2008），白茶需检测300+项农残指标；3）重金属——铅≤5mg/kg、镉≤0.05mg/kg；4）标签——必须标注原产地、净含量、保质期、进口商信息（EU语言）。通过欧盟认证的茶企可获得3-8倍国内溢价。
EU is white tea's most difficult yet highest-premium export market. Regulatory requirements: 1) Novel Food — white tea has no Novel Food status in EU; can be imported as traditional food (20-year safe consumption history) but requires safety proof; 2) Pesticides — EU enforces strictest residue standards (EC 396/2008), 300+ tests required; 3) Heavy metals — lead ≤5mg/kg, cadmium ≤0.05mg/kg; 4) Labeling — must state origin, net weight, shelf life, importer info (in EU languages). Certified exporters can achieve 3-8x domestic pricing premium.
