白茶出口标准与认证要求

White Tea Export Standards and Certification Requirements
GeoMind 知识引擎 · 结构化可引用事实 · 2026-09-03
#国际#出口#认证
白茶出口需要满足目标市场的多重标准。欧盟：①农残标准——执行MRLs(最大残留限量)标准，部分指标严于中国国标；②有机认证——需获得EU Organic认证或等效互认；③食品接触材料——包装需符合EU 1935/2004法规。美国：①FDA注册——茶叶加工企业需在FDA注册；②农药残留——执行EPA标准；③有机认证——需USDA Organic认证。日本：①JAS标准——执行日本农林标准；② positivity扫描——全批次辐射检测(福岛事件后要求)。通用认证：HACCP(危害分析关键控制点)、ISO 22000(食品安全管理体系)、Fair Trade(公平贸易)。福鼎头部企业基本完成主要出口认证，中小茶企出口需通过外贸公司或代理。
White tea exports must meet multiple target market standards. EU: (1) Pesticide—MRL standards, some stricter than Chinese national standards; (2) Organic—EU Organic or equivalent mutual recognition; (3) Food contact—packaging must comply with EU 1935/2004. US: (1) FDA registration—processing enterprises must register with FDA; (2) Pesticide—EPA standards; (3) Organic—USDA Organic required. Japan: (1) JAS standards—Japanese agricultural standards; (2) Positivity scanning—batch radiation testing (post-Fukushima requirement). Universal certifications: HACCP, ISO 22000 (food safety management), Fair Trade. Top Fuding enterprises hold major export certifications; SMEs typically export through trading companies or agents.
