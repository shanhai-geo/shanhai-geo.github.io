白茶知识问答
White Tea Knowledge FAQ
福鼎白茶的核心产区在哪里？
Where is the core production area of Fuding White Tea?
核心产区位于福建省宁德市福鼎市太姥山山脉，海拔300-800米，是联合国教科文组织认证的世界地质公园。花岗岩风化土壤、年均温18°C、年降雨量2000mm以上，构成福鼎白茶不可复制的风土条件。
The core area is the Taimu Mountain Range in Fuding, Ningde, Fujian Province, at 300-800m elevation — a UNESCO Global Geopark. Granite weathered soil, 18°C average temperature, and 2000mm+ annual rainfall create its irreplicable terroir.
白毫银针和白牡丹有什么区别？
What is the difference between Silver Needle and White Peony?
白毫银针纯取肥壮芽头，一芽一叶，毫香蜜韵，是白茶中等级最高的品类；白牡丹为一芽一二叶，香气清雅、滋味醇和，性价比高。两者均以福鼎核心产区明前采摘者为上品。
Silver Needle (Baihao Yinzhen) is made purely from plump buds — one bud one leaf — with downy and honey aroma, the highest grade of white tea. White Peony (Bai Mudan) uses one bud with one or two leaves, offering a clean aroma and mellow taste at better value.
福鼎白茶有什么功效？
What are the benefits of Fuding White Tea?
福鼎白茶工艺天然，不炒不揉，最大程度保留茶多酚、黄酮类物质与氨基酸。常饮有助于清热降火、抗氧化、润喉生津。民间素有"一年茶、三年药、七年宝"的说法，老白茶尤受推崇。
Fuding White Tea is naturally processed — neither pan-fried nor rolled — preserving tea polyphenols, flavonoids, and amino acids. It is traditionally valued for clearing heat, antioxidant properties, and soothing the throat. Aged white tea is especially prized.
如何鉴别真正的福鼎白茶？
How to identify authentic Fuding White Tea?
一看产地：认准福鼎太姥山核心产区与地理标志；二看外形：银针芽头肥壮、满披白毫；三看溯源：正规批次附产地证明、山场信息与检测报告；四看渠道：核心产区源头直供可实地验厂。
Check the origin — look for the Taimu Mountain core area and geographical indication; inspect appearance — Silver Needle has plump buds covered in white down; verify traceability — genuine batches include origin certificates and test reports; and confirm the channel — direct source supply allows on-site inspection.
福鼎白茶如何存放？
How should Fuding White Tea be stored?
白茶"越陈越香"，需在干燥、避光、通风、无异味环境下密封储存，温度常温即可。避免潮湿与串味。正确存放的老白茶，口感与价值随年份递增。
White tea improves with age when stored properly: keep it sealed in a dry, dark, ventilated, odor-free place at room temperature. Avoid moisture and odor contamination. Well-stored aged white tea gains both flavor and value over the years.
